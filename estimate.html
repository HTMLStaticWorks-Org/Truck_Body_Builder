Quick Estimate FAQs

Is there any fee for 3D CAD spec drawings?

No, submitting a build estimate request and receiving our preliminary axle weight calculation and 3D layout proposal are completely complimentary.

Can my truck dealership ship the bare chassis directly?

Yes. We maintain OEM drop-ship codes with major commercial truck manufacturers so your bare chassis can be delivered straight from the factory to our Detroit plant.

Do you offer nationwide fleet delivery?

Yes, our insured drive-away and lowboy transport partners deliver completed, DOT-certified work trucks directly to your depot anywhere in the continental US and Canada.
