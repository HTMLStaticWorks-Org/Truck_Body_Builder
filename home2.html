Fleet Operational Risks

Common Threats to Commercial Truck Uptime & Payload

Off-the-shelf mass-produced truck bodies suffer from weak welds and poor corrosion protection, exposing fleet operators to costly repairs and downtime.

Subframe Cracking & Fatigue

Inferior stitch welds and undersized crossmembers fracture under heavy torsion and rough jobsite terrain.

Corrosion & Salt Rust-Out

Untreated mild-steel underbodies corrode rapidly from winter road de-icers, shortening vehicle service life.

Excess Body Deadweight

Over-heavy legacy steel bodies eat into legal GVWR limits, reducing revenue-generating cargo capacity.

Hydraulic & PTO Failures

Exposed wiring harnesses and uncalibrated PTO hydraulic pumps cause costly roadside breakdowns every season.
