Build Workflow

Our 4-Step Truck Body Fabrication Process

We follow a rigorous, ISO-certified engineering workflow to transform bare commercial chassis into road-ready, high-payload fleet assets.

STEP 01

Chassis Audit & CAD

Measuring cab-to-axle dimensions, axle weight distribution, and 3D structural CAD blueprints.

STEP 02

CNC & Jig Welding

Precision fiber-laser steel cutting, press-brake forming, and AWS-certified structural welding.

STEP 03

Hydraulics & Wiring

Installing PTO pumps, hoist cylinders, multiplexed LED harnesses, and heavy liftgate systems.

STEP 04

Paint & DOT Sign-Off

Baked polyurethane finish, load testing, FMVSS compliance inspection, and final delivery.
