Our 3-Phase Build Process

What Happens During Your Custom Truck Body Build

We follow a disciplined, ISO-audited manufacturing workflow from chassis arrival to final DOT certification.

STEP 1

Chassis Intake & 3D CAD Blueprinting

We inspect your bare chassis, verify frame rail dimensions and PTO clearances, and generate 3D SolidWorks blueprints with axle weight distribution charts for your sign-off.

STEP 2

CNC Fabrication & Hydraulic Mounting

Our AWS-certified welders assemble the structural subframe and body panels, mount the shear plates to the chassis, and plumb all PTO hydraulics, cranes, and liftgates.

STEP 3

Industrial Coating & DOT Certification

Every completed body undergoes zinc-epoxy priming, baked polyurethane painting, FMVSS 108 LED harness testing, and NTEA weight certification before fleet handoff.
