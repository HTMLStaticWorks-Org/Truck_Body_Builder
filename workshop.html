Founded in 2011

Advancing Heavy-Duty Truck Body Fabrication & Fleet Engineering

TitanRig was established with a clear mission: to bridge the gap between aerospace-grade structural engineering and heavy commercial truck upfitting. Our founders saw how mass-produced, thin-gauge truck bodies suffered from premature frame cracking, rust-out, and excessive tare weight.

CAD/FEA Precision

Every body design undergoes 3D Finite Element stress testing and axle weight calculation.

Heavy-Duty Steel

We fabricate with Hardox 450 wear plate, A36 structural steel, and 6061-T6 extruded aluminum.

Fleet Profitability

We reduce body tare weight to maximize legal cargo payload while extending service life.

Our Engineering Methodology

A Custom Structural Blueprint for Every Commercial Chassis

We recognize that every route, cargo profile, and jobsite terrain demands a tailored engineering solution. Extreme point loads, corrosive road salts, and high-cycle liftgate operations cannot be handled by generic light-duty bodies.

Our engineers integrate 3D chassis modeling, custom subframe shear plates, and high-output PTO hydraulics to build truck bodies that outlast the chassis underneath them.
